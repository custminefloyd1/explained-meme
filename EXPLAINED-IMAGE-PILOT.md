# Explained image-rights pilot: first 10

This pilot covers the first ten staged Explained entries. It does not clear or publish any image.

## Result

- **0 cleared for upload**
- **1 direct creator licensing route:** This Is Fine
- **6 identifiable studio/licensor routes:** Is This a Pigeon?, One Does Not Simply, Ancient Aliens, Futurama Fry, Surprised Pikachu and Mocking SpongeBob
- **3 owner/contact investigations still incomplete:** Success Kid, Bad Luck Brian and Philosoraptor

Finding a rights holder or inbox is not permission. Every entry remains blocked until the exact asset, use, attribution, territory, term and fee are confirmed in writing.

## Recommended pilot order

1. **This Is Fine** — request terms directly from K.C. Green.
2. **One Does Not Simply** — use Warner Bros. Discovery's formal still-licensing route to learn whether this type of small editorial site use is commercially viable.
3. **Success Kid** — verify Laney Griner's current authorised contact before sending any request.
4. **Bad Luck Brian** and **Philosoraptor** — resolve ownership/contact before proceeding.
5. Treat the five remaining entertainment stills as a separate studio-licensing group; these are likely slower and may be uneconomic for the site.

## Approval evidence required

For an entry to change to `cleared_editorial`, retain all of the following:

- the exact approved image or an approved source-file URL;
- the copyright owner/licensor's identity;
- written permission or licence terms;
- the exact attribution wording;
- permitted medium (commercial, ad-supported website editorial page);
- territory and duration;
- fee and payment evidence, if applicable;
- confirmation that Screensaver, downloads, merchandise, standalone galleries and advertising are excluded unless separately licensed.

## Safer fallback

If licensing is declined, unaffordable or unanswered, commission a genuinely original explanatory illustration. Do not trace, redraw or closely imitate the protected character, photograph, frame, pose or composition.
